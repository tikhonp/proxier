Linden & Birch

Solid wood furniture, made slowly and made to last.

We are a two-person workshop building tables, benches and shelving to order. Everything is cut from locally sourced oak, ash and birch, joined without screws where we can manage it, and finished with hard wax oil that is easy to repair at home.

Most pieces take six to ten weeks from the first sketch to delivery. We make a handful of things each season rather than a catalogue, so every order starts with a conversation about the room it is going into.
